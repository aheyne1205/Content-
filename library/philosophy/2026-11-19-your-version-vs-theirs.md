## Reel script (30–60s)
Ever had a connection that's powerful in person
and then over text it's like a different person?

That messed with my head.

Then it clicked.
The gap wasn't someone doing something wrong.
The gap was between what I believed connection should look like
and what they believed it should look like.

The world we see isn't reality.
It's our perspective of it.
We believe what we believe
because of our thoughts and how we were brought up.

So stop asking "why won't they do it my way?"
Start asking "what do I actually need?"

## Caption
Ever had a connection that's strong in person, then over text it feels like nothing? It messed with my head for a while.

Then it clicked. The gap wasn't anyone doing anything wrong. It was the difference between what I believed connection should look like and what the other person believed it should look like.

The world we see isn't reality. It's our perspective of it, built from our thoughts and how we were raised. Two people can share the same moment and live two completely different versions of it.

The work isn't getting people to see it your way. It's understanding the gap and getting clear on what you need.

Ever been on a completely different page to someone you really liked?
