## Reel script (30–60s)
There's a moment on this journey nobody warns you about.

Life hits you harder than it ever has.
You feel powerless. Weak. Defenceless.

And right there, a door opens.
The exit.

It tells you you've done enough.
A little bit of work. Enough to get by.
You convince yourself you can survive and thrive.

But you know.
Deep down in your soul, you know you haven't.

That moment, where you feel the weakest?
That's where your strength is forged.

Don't take the door.

## Caption
There's a moment on the road of self-improvement that nobody talks about.

Life hits you harder than before. You feel powerless, weak, defenceless. And right then, a door opens. The early exit. It tells you you've done enough work to get by. You convince yourself you can survive and thrive.

But deep down in your soul, you know you haven't done the work.

I've stood at that door. What I've learned is that the moment you feel weakest is the exact moment your strength is being forged. Walk out early and you miss the whole point.

Have you ever taken the exit early? Be honest with yourself.
