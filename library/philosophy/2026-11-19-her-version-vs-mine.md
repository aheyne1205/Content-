# Your reality is just your perspective.
Pillar: philosophy · Format: Running/training reel · Status: idea
Source dump: 29q48srl8u8urhjhgoa2

## Hooks
- Your reality is just your perspective.
- Her version of connection was different to mine. Neither was wrong.
- In person it was electric. Over text, nothing.

## Reel script (30–60s)
When we catch up in person,
the connection is powerful.
The eye contact. All of it.

Over text? Totally different.

And I had to sit with that as a man.
The gap wasn't her doing something wrong.
The gap was between what I believed things should be
and what she believed things should be.

The world we see isn't reality.
It's our perspective of it.
We believe what we believe
because of our thoughts and how we were brought up.

So I stopped asking "why isn't she doing it my way?"
and started asking "what do I actually need?"

## Caption
In person, the connection is strong. Over text, it's a completely different story. For a while that messed with my head.

Then it clicked. The gap wasn't her doing anything wrong. It was the difference between what I believed connection should look like and what she believed it should look like.

The world we see isn't reality. It's our perspective of it, built from our thoughts and how we were raised. Two people can share the same moment and live two different versions of it.

As a man, my job isn't to make everyone see it my way. It's to understand the gap and decide what I need.

Have you ever been on a completely different page to someone you really liked?
