## Reel script (30–60s)
If I like you, I'll text you back straight away.
I'm not in the fucking industry of playing games.

But here's what I caught myself doing.
I'd send a message
and then sit there anticipating the reply.

Eight, nine, ten hours later.
Every single time.

And it threw the whole equilibrium of my life off balance.
Tightness in my chest.
Chasing a feeling.

It wasn't jealousy.
I just liked her.
And I was chasing something back that wasn't coming the way I wanted it.

The moment I asked myself
"what the fuck am I doing with my time?"
that's when the clarity came.

That's on me to own. Not her.

## Caption
I text back straight away if I like you. No games, no waiting 23 hours to look cool. That's just me.

But I caught myself sending a message and then sitting in it. Anticipating the reply. Eight, nine, ten hours later, every time. And while I waited, my whole day went off balance. Tightness in the chest. Chasing that feeling of it being mutual.

It wasn't jealousy. I liked her. I wanted it to feel even. It didn't, and that's not her fault. That's mine to own.

The question that snapped me out of it: what the fuck am I doing with my time?

What are you chasing right now that's costing you your peace?
