## Reel script (30–60s)
If I like someone, I text back straight away.
I'm not in the fucking industry of playing games.

But here's what I caught myself doing.
Send a message.
Then sit there anticipating the reply.

Eight, nine, ten hours.
Tightness in my chest.
The whole equilibrium of my life thrown off balance.

It wasn't jealousy.
I liked them, and I was chasing that feeling of it being mutual.

If that's you right now, ask yourself what I asked myself:
"What the fuck am I doing with my time?"

Your peace can't hang on someone else's reply time.
That one's on us to own.

## Caption
If I like someone, I text back straight away. No games, no waiting 23 hours to look cool. That's just me.

But I caught myself sending a message and then sitting in it. Anticipating the reply. Hours going by. Tightness in the chest. My whole day off balance, all because I was chasing the feeling of it being mutual.

It wasn't jealousy. It was me handing my peace to someone else's phone.

If you're in that spot right now, ask yourself the question that snapped me out of it: what am I actually doing with my time?

What are you chasing right now that's costing you your peace?
