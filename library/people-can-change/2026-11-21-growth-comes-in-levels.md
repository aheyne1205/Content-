# You think you've passed the test. Then life sends it back harder.

## Hooks

- Growth isn't a road. It's levels.
- Just when you think you've done the work, life checks.

## Reel script (30–60s)
Growth doesn't come all at once.
It comes in waves. In levels.

First you don't even know what the road looks like.
Or how to start the engine.

Then you're driving, and the challenges start coming.
And I reckon that's on purpose.
To grow, you've got to know yourself first.

You pass a level.
You feel like you've conquered it.

Then life hands you the same test again.
Tougher. Longer.
And you realise you hadn't done the work.

That's not failure.
That's the next level.
Keep driving.

## Caption
Self-improvement isn't a straight road. It comes in levels.

First it's hard just to see what the road looks like or how to start the engine. Then you start driving, and the challenges come. I truly believe that's intentional. To grow, you have to know yourself first.

Here's the part that got me. I'd feel like I'd conquered an area. My reactions, my ego, my patience. Then life would hand me a similar test, only tougher and longer. And I'd realise I hadn't actually done the work yet.

That isn't going backwards. That's the next level checking if you're ready.

What test does life keep sending you?
