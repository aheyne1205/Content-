# Content Library

A place to brain dump the raw stuff (lessons, feelings, problems I solved, thoughts from the past) and turn it into Instagram content without polishing the life out of it.

**Live app:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DvufVb7XNUjkStZvAfNpNL (private until you share it)
Source: `app/index.html`

## How to use it

1. **Dump.** Open the app on your phone. Tap the mic and talk, or type. Pick what kind of dump it is if you want to (lesson, feeling, problem → solution, past thought, something you read).
2. **Sort.** Hit *Sort into content*. One dump can become several pieces. Each piece gets:
   - 3 hooks (the first 2 seconds)
   - a 30–60s reel script, written the way you talk
   - a caption with hashtags
   - 5–7 carousel slides
   - a quote-card line
   - a Story prompt or poll
3. **Film.** Go to the Library, filter by pillar, film it raw. Mark it *Filmed*, then *Posted*.
4. **No time?** Hit *Save raw for later* and sort it whenever.

Recording on a run with a clip mic: use your phone's voice memo transcription, then paste the text into the dump box.

## Safety

Anything about suicide or self-harm is written around surviving and hope, never methods, and the caption ends with Lifeline (13 11 14). Instagram also limits reach on posts that break safe-messaging guidelines, so this protects the content as well as the people watching it.
